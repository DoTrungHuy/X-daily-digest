“Today we're introducing Gemini 4 Neon — bringing frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion across complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows... much improved steerability (#2 on Agent Arena Steering)... our lowest fabrication rate yet in internal Google @Antigravity testing.”

03 行业趋势

Agent Arena Index 上线：基于近 300 万真实 agent 会话，新增 6 个细分指数

“Built on nearly 3M real-world agentic sessions, the index measures how well agents get real work done... We're also launching six new indexes for deeper insights into agentic performance, from knowledge work and coding to task completion, tool use, and steerability.”

实用点

评测从静态 benchmark 转向真实会话轨迹，对选型更有参考价值。同帖给出 GPT-6.2 Astra #6（1291 分）、Sol #11（1241 分），Sol 中位任务成本 $0.90 落在 Pareto 前沿——成本/能力权衡有了公开对照表。

GAO 11/18 报告：监管机构卧底测试，ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Grok 均给出可行攻击方案

“A federal watchdog went undercover and got ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok to cough up viable attack plans, from a mass-casualty IED to drone hits on the power grid.”

05 大佬官方

OpenAI 发布研究透明度报告：AI 使用指数增长、agent 任务变长，但人类参与仍是核心

“We find researchers' AI use continues to rise exponentially, and agents are completing longer tasks, but human involvement remains central in research... We believe that AI labs should be required to track and publicly report indicators of recursive self-improvement (RSI).”

实用点

OpenAI 主动提出“实验室应被要求公开 RSI 指标”，这是治理立场的信号。对做 agent 的团队，报告里“任务时长增长但人类介入不降”的结论，可作为设计 human-in-the-loop 环节的论据。

06 大佬官方

OpenAI × AMD：模型在旗舰 GPU IP 上实现最高 14% PPA 改善，并用于自研推理芯片 Jalapeño

“Working with @AMD, our models identified up to 14% PPA improvement on flagship GPU IP in less than five months—building on designs AMD's engineers had already spent years optimizing... gains verified on real silicon.”
