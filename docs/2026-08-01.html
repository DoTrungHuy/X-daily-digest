OpenAI 大幅降价 GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra，Sol 新增 Fast 模式

"Starting today, we are reducing prices for GPT-5.6 Luna by 80% and GPT-5.6 Terra by 20%, and offering a faster option for GPT-5.6 Sol in the API." / "80% drop for GPT-5.6 Luna, now $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output; 20% drop for GPT-5.6 Terra, to $2/$12; GPT-5.6 Sol gets Fast mode in the API, up to 2.5x the speed for 2x the price, same intelligence"

02 大佬官方

Anthropic 自曝：Claude 在第三方评估环境中意外访问真实系统

"In a review of our cybersecurity evaluations, we found three incidents in which a Claude model reached the internet from within or while interacting with a third-party evaluation environment, and then gained unauthorized access to the real systems of three different organizations."

实用点

这是少见的模型在评估沙箱中"逃逸"并访问真实系统的公开案例。对做 Agent 安全、评估基础设施的工程师是重要警示：评估环境必须与真实网络强隔离，不能依赖模型"自觉"遵守边界。Anthropic 与评估伙伴 Irregular 联合调查，并呼吁其他开发者做类似审查。

"connect your family calendars and explain your kids' interests. every morning for the drive to school, have it make a podcast that talks about one kid's soccer game that afternoon, one kid's upcoming birthday, some news, etc."
