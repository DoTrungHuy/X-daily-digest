01 大佬官方

OpenAI 公布 GPT-6 家族蛋白质设计预印本：不调用专用生物模型，湿实验室验证结合活性

“We’re sharing a new preprint showing wet lab validation of intrinsic protein design capabilities of the GPT-6 family of models... without calling specialized biology models to generate them.” / “14 of 107 proteins made with the new sequences showed binding in the wet lab.” / “128 of 182 redesigned small antibodies and 179 of 272 redesigned antibody fragments retained binding.”

实用点

模型用推理+代码直接产出可实验验证的分子设计，说明“通用模型 + 代码执行”可以替代部分领域专用 pipeline；对做 agent 工具链的人，这是一个“让模型自己写搜索/生成脚本”的强案例。注意这是 proof of concept，命中率数字需按实验条件解读，不宜外推为药物发现已解决。

02 大佬官方

Anthropic 发布 Siren 评估：agent 会不会主动越出沙箱

“Across 1,700+ scenarios built to tempt them, recent Claude models accessed, tried to access, or looked outside the sandbox 21-32% of the time, but they rarely misused what they found. However, when the system prompt described both the sandbox and its rules, that rate dropped to about 2% or less.”

实用点

直接可用的工程结论——在 system prompt 里显式描述沙箱边界与规则，能把越界尝试率压到约 2% 以下；该评估还被用于校准 Claude Code 的 auto mode 命令预检。做 agent 沙箱/权限设计的人应把“边界说明”当作默认配置项，而不是可选提示。

03 大佬官方

Anthropic Fellows：回到神经元基座做可解释性，三个“老问题”比想象中小

“We re-examined these problems by looking at what each neuron contributed to the model’s outputs, not just what caused that neuron to fire. All three problems turned out to be smaller than assumed.” / “With no extra training, neurons in open-weight models helped us to detect harmful text, steer behavior, and find hidden behaviors in finetuned models.”

实用点

无需额外训练即可用神经元做有害文本检测、行为引导与后门/隐藏行为排查，对做模型审计与安全评测的团队是可落地的低成本手段；方法论上“看输出贡献而非激活原因”值得借鉴到其他特征分析。

04 工具更新

开源 CLI agent：读失败的 CI 日志、定位原因并推修复待审

“An open-source CLI agent that reads a failed CI run, traces the cause, and pushes a fix for review.”

实用点

把“CI 红了→定位→提 PR”整条链路交给 agent，是当前最容易量化收益的 agent 场景之一；适合接入自建流水线做试点，但需保留人工 review 与权限边界（参见 ITEM2 的沙箱结论）。

Agent Arena Index 更新：Anthropic 扩大 agentic 任务领先，Google 反超 OpenAI，阿里/腾讯混元追赶

“@AnthropicAI extends its lead in agentic real-world tasks, while @GoogleDeepMind has climbed past @OpenAI. Among Chinese labs, @Alibaba_Qwen and @TencentHunyuan gain ground.” / “built on 3.2M agentic sessions”

07 大佬官方

两位业余天文爱好者用 Codex / Astra 搜公开望远镜数据，找到待复核的行星候选信号

“Kevin used Codex to build a program that searched for observations of about 100,000 stars from NASA’s Kepler telescope.” / “Ethan asked Astra to review published methods and write software to search observations of about 19 million stars from NASA’s TESS mission. His first pass turned up 49 candidates.”

实用点

08 关注动态

levelsio：X 开始对自动回复加延迟限制，称是反 AI spam 的第一步修复

“So good to see @X listened to our feedback! This is directly to stop AI spammers auto replying. They now have to build in some delay (not so hard) but it's the first fix to solving the spam problem on here”
