01 关注动态

Grok Bot 获“非开发者”用户盛赞：一周内成为业务首席参谋

“Grok Bot is hands down one of the best products I have ever used. Maybe the best ever. I have had it less than a week. I am not a developer. I talk to one chat and it already knows my business better than me.”

实用点

对 agent 开发者而言，这是理解“非技术用户如何定义 agent 价值”的绝佳案例。用户强调的不是代码能力，而是“读取 Slack/邮件/Notion/Stripe 并主动简报”、“从销售通话中提炼规则并自动执行”、“一键在 Stripe 创建限时折扣码”。这提示 agent 产品设计应优先打通业务数据源与工作流自动化，而非堆砌模型能力。

OpenAI 首批 NVIDIA Vera Rubin 机架上线，用于下一代前沿预训练

“A milestone for our infrastructure: our first NVIDIA Vera Rubin racks are here and now running our training stack. This is an important step as we expand compute that powers OpenAI's next generation of frontier AI pre-training.”

GPT-5.6 Sol 限时降价 50%，Codex 与 Copilot 同步生效

“📣 GPT-5.6 Sol is 50% cheaper through September 3. A high reasoning ceiling for complex codebases and demanding, long-running agentic work. Give it a try in @code today!”

直接的成本利好。对于依赖 GPT-5.6 Sol 处理复杂代码库或长时运行 agent 任务的团队，9 月 3 日前是迁移或扩容的窗口期。同时该模型已接入 GitHub Copilot，意味着在 Copilot 工作流中也能享受降价，值得立即验证性价比。

04 行业趋势

GLM-5.3 (Max) 登顶 Code Arena: WebDev 开源 Pareto 前沿

“GLM-5.3 (Max) would sit at #2 among open models (upon open weights release, and #8 overall) with 1597 pts in the Code Arena: WebDev. At $3.65/M, GLM-5.3 (Max) is on par with Qwen3.8 (Max) ($5.00/M) and out performs Gemini-3.7-flash-high ($2.86/M) and Deepseek-v4-flash-high ($1.10/M).”

实用点

开源模型竞争加剧。GLM-5.3 (Max) 在 WebDev 任务上以 $3.65/M 的价格点提供了超越 Gemini-3.7-flash-high 和 DeepSeek-V4-flash-high 的性能，性价比显著。对于在 Web 开发场景中寻求开源替代方案的团队，这是一个值得 benchmark 的新选项。

05 工具更新

Notion 发布团队级 Skills：从“个人技能”到“组织记忆”

“Skills for your whole team are here. Most agents start from scratch. Skills teach them how your team already works. Ask your Notion Agent to turn your team’s best work into a skill… then share it, let your agent load it automatically, or use it with your local agents.”

实用点

Notion 将 Skills 从个人扩展至团队，并支持“自动使用”模式（agent 根据任务自动匹配技能，无需斜杠命令）。结合此前“Skills 重塑终端用户编程”的观点，这标志着 agent 工作流从“手动调用工具”向“隐式上下文注入”演进。开发者可关注其与本地 agent 的互操作性。

06 其他

Notion 员工实战分享：用 Skill 从会议记录生成架构图

“my favorite notion skill: /generate architecture diagrams from meeting notes. i made a skill that generates a lightweight whiteboard HTML artifact based on conversations i have with a customer.”

实用点

一个具体的 agent 应用范式：将非结构化的客户会议转化为可视化白板，辅助需求对齐。作者指出“it's not perfect but it's a good starting point for complex conversations”，提示这类“初稿生成器”是当前 agent 落地的高价值场景——降低沟通成本，而非追求完美输出。

07 行业趋势

Grok 参与设计个性化 mRNA 癌症疫苗，催生新公司 Gamgee

“The final vaccine construct for Rose was designed by Grok. AI wasn't just helping with research here...Grok was part of designing the actual treatment. Her tumors significantly shrank after treatment. Paul just launched Gamgee and raised $4 MILLION in seed funding led by Founders Fund.”

实用点

AI 从“辅助研究”走向“参与设计治疗方案”的典型案例。虽然是个例，但展示了 agent 在生物信息学中的潜在应用边界。对 CS 开发者而言，这提示跨学科 agent 应用（如设计蛋白质、疫苗构建）正在从实验走向商业化，相关工具链（如生物序列生成、验证）可能成为新方向。
