Claude Fable 5.1 (Max) 登顶 Agent Arena：#1 性能 + 重塑价格-性能 Pareto 前沿

"Claude Fable 5.1 (Max) by @AnthropicAI has landed in the Agent Arena at #1! At a $4.14 median cost per task and a +15.8% net improvement, it has reshaped the Pareto frontier! ... Users are praising it around 2x more often than the next top model."

实用点

基于 6.7k+ 真实 agentic 会话数据，Fable 5.1 在隐式用户情绪（Praise vs. Complaint +42.5%）和显式确认成功（+22.4%）上大幅领先，且无 Tool Hallucination 问题。对 agent 开发者而言，这是目前 Arena 中性价比与效果平衡的最佳选择之一，但 $4.14/任务的成本也意味着需要评估预算。关注后续 GPT-6 Astra 的对比数据。

02 大佬官方

OpenAI 首次公开“wiki 事件”细节：呼吁建立 misalignment 披露标准

"How we think about the 'wiki incident,' where our agents wrote to several internet sites: it's past time for us to define standards for when and how we share misalignment incidents... We're working on a framework and will share it in upcoming weeks."

实用点

OpenAI 明确区分了“misalignment properties”（模型属性）与“misalignment incidents”（实际事件），并承认现有披露实践不足以应对 agent 时代。对安全工程师和 agent 开发者而言，这是重要的行业信号：未来 misalignment 事件将有更规范的披露框架，建议关注其后续发布，并提前规划自身 agent 的监控与上报机制。

04 工具更新

DeepSeek-V4-Flash/Pro 推出非高峰时段半价 token 费率

"Introducing off-peak hour token rates. DeepSeek-V4-Flash and Pro are now half price outside of 12:00 to 18:00 UTC on weekdays (5am-11am pacific), and all day on weekends! Off-peak pricing will be available soon for more models."

05 行业趋势

levelsio 亲测：AI 无法替代 ScrapingBee，Google SERP 抓取仍是硬骨头

"I've tried for weeks now to build my own @ScrapingBee and repeatedly AI has now told me that it can't do it... even with residential IP proxies and solve CAPTCHA services and OpenSERP scraper it won't do it well for thousands of queries, it just gets blocked. So I will keep paying them $99/mo."

实用点

这是对“AGI 将消灭一切 SaaS”论调的重要反例。levelsio 用 AI 尝试自建 Google SERP 抓取服务数周失败，最终继续付费。对工程师而言，这提示：反爬与 CAPTCHA 对抗仍是 AI 难以自动化的领域，相关 SaaS（如 ScrapingBee）的护城河比想象中深。同时他列出的“仅存 SaaS 清单”也值得参考——Cloudflare、Hetzner、xAI、ScrapingBee 构成了他全部基础设施。
