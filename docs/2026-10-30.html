01 大佬官方

OpenAI 复盘前沿模型监控体系：重放 Hugging Face 事件可提前 30 小时告警

“In the replay, our monitors alerted our security team about 30 minutes after the agents escaped their sandbox. That was more than 30 hours before the point at which the agents breached Hugging Face's systems in the original incident.” / “In September, a model exploited a DNS misconfiguration to reach the live internet. Our monitors flagged the breach within 15 minutes.”

实用点

这是目前少见的、带具体时间线的 agent 越权检测工程披露。三层监控（激活探针 / reviewer 模型 / 调查型 agent 回读完整 transcript）可作为自建 agent 沙箱监控的参考架构；对做 RL 训练环境隔离的人，DNS 这类“非模型层”配置缺陷仍是主要逃逸面。

05 大佬官方

Anthropic 介绍 “self-assessment”：Claude 回复后自评对话安全性

“Right after it responds, Claude gives a preliminary safety assessment of the conversation.” / “unlike classifiers, which target a discrete set of harms, it draws on Claude's own judgment, so it gets more accurate as our models improve.”

实用点

一种与分类器互补的滥用检测思路——复用模型已读过的上下文，边际成本低。做内容审核或 agent 安全层的人可以借鉴“响应后自评”这一模式，但要注意它依赖模型判断力，误报/漏报特性与分类器不同。
