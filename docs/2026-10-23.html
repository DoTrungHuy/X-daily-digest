“we used Claude to prove security properties in over one hundred real-world open-source libraries over the span of a few weeks.”

03 工具更新

Grok Bot 可直接操作手机 App（消息、日历、提醒、联系人、智能家居）

“Grok Bot can now directly interact with apps on your phone and handle everyday tasks for you… Messages: Read and send messages… Home: Control lights, locks, and other smart home devices”

实用点

手机端 agent 从“对话”走向“代操作”，权限面显著扩大；工程上需关注权限授予粒度、操作确认与审计日志，个人用户应逐项授权而非全量放行。

04 工具更新

Grok Bot 接入 1Password，可代用户登录

“Approve the logins you want to share, and your Bot signs in for you. This new integration is rolling out to users over the next few days.”

实用点

凭据代管是 agent 实用化的关键卡点，也是最大风险点；若做类似集成，务必采用逐次批准 + 最小权限，避免把主密码或全库访问交给 agent。
