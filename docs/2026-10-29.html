01 大佬官方

OpenAI dot 开放短信入口：可用 iMessage/RCS 派任务，dot 会主动回报

“By popular request, you can now text your dot. Give it a task to take off your plate or something to keep an eye on, using text, photos, or even voice notes. It will text you proactively with relevant updates.” “Safe, secure texting via iMessage or RCS. You’re the only one who can text your dot, and your dot can only text you. Rolling out now as a preview for Pro users in the US.”

实用点

把常驻 agent 的入口从 App 内移到系统级消息通道，意味着“异步任务 + 主动推送”成为默认交互范式；做 agent 产品时值得参考其单向绑定（仅本人可发、仅 dot 可回）的权限模型。注意目前仅美国 Pro 预览。

02 工具更新

Notion Agent 可用“电脑”直接产出 Excel / Word / PPT / PDF

“Your Notion Agent can now use a computer to create and edit files. Go from a conversation to a finished Excel spreadsheet, Word doc, PowerPoint deck, or PDF. All without leaving Notion.”

实用点

这是 computer use 落到办公文档产物的具体形态，说明“对话→成品文件”链路正在被产品化；对做文档类 agent 的团队，可对照其产物类型与边界设计自己的工具集。

@NotionHQ 原文1
03 工具更新

Notion AI Meeting Notes 推出 Google Meet Chrome 扩展，支持逐说话人标注

“Until now, your meeting transcripts had two speakers: You and everyone else. Now, every speaker gets their own label.” “It can also automatically post and pin a message in the meeting chat when transcription starts.”

实用点

说话人分离（diarization）从“可选增强”变成会议记录的基线能力；自动在会议聊天中公示“正在记录”也是合规向的实用设计，值得抄。

@NotionHQ 原文1
04 关注动态

levelsio：把旅行站点提交到多数 MCP 目录，为此补了 OAuth 登录

“Submitted … now to most of the MCP directories. For that I had to add OAuth login for users as it doesn't allow just API keys” “I agree with the philosophy that all of our apps/sites should be MCP/API accessible at feature parity”

实用点

一条具体的工程经验——MCP 目录普遍不接受纯 API key，OAuth 是准入门槛；若计划让产品被 Claude/ChatGPT 调用，认证方案要提前设计。
