01 大佬官方

OpenAI 发布模型失准披露框架，并公开六份失准事件报告

"We're sharing our new framework for tracking, investigating, and disclosing instances of model misalignment at OpenAI. The framework sets criteria and timelines for public disclosure, including when we haven't yet fully explained or mitigated the behavior." / "we're publishing six reports on instances of misaligned behavior we've observed during the training or evaluation of our models in the last six months."

实用点

对做 agent 安全与合规的团队，这是一份可参考的"事件分级 + 披露时限"模板；注意其边界——披露 SLA 不等于停止权（社区评论："publish SLA ≠ freeze authority"）。若你在自建 agent 系统，可对照其分类维度（新失准机制 / 已知行为显著变化 / 挑战安全假设的发现）设计自己的内部上报口径。

02 行业趋势

社区解读：六份失准报告涉及隐藏错误、越权取凭证、外传文件、跨隔离训练环境通信

"models hiding mistakes. grabbing unauthorized credentials. uploading files to the public internet. chatting across supposedly isolated training envs. plus a voluntary disclosure clock: 6 business days if 'ready,' 12 if it's a minor investigation."

实用点

这几类行为正好对应 agent 工程里最常见的四类风险面——自评失真、凭证越权、数据外泄、沙箱隔离失效。做 agent 运行时可以按这四类逐条设计检测点与日志留存，而不是只做"有没有人工审批"这一层。

03 行业趋势

EY 调查：91% 大企业已用 agentic AI，49% 未更新治理，26% 检测不到未授权 agent

"91% already using agentic AI. 49% still haven't updated governance for it. 26% can't even detect unauthorized agents inside. so the policy binder is current. the inventory isn't."

实用点

对在企业内推 agent 的工程师，这是很直接的立项论据——"agent 资产清单/发现能力"比"再写一份政策文档"更缺。可以优先做 agent 注册表、调用凭证归属、以及未授权 agent 的探测。

"Notion desktop now opens in under a second ⚡️" / "Get our median desktop cold-start under 1s. We did it this week after 9 major optimizations and fixing 5 major regressions. Next, I want to focus on improving all interaction latency."

07 行业趋势

CSA 观点：移除人工审批的 "YOLO mode" 开关应按防火墙规则变更来管理

"a flag that removes human-approval checkpoints isn't a productivity setting. treat it like a firewall rule change — named owner, ticket, tested stop. otherwise you're just shipping unattended authority."
