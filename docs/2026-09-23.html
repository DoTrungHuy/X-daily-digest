01 大佬官方

OpenAI 发布 GPT-6 Sol 与 GPT-6 Luna，API 价格较 GPT-5.6 促销价再降 50%

"Please welcome GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna to the GPT-6 universe." / "GPT-6 Sol and Luna build on the advances behind GPT-6 Astra, bringing much of its strengths into faster and more affordable models to support work at scale." / "50% lower API prices for Sol and Luna compared with GPT‑5.6 promotional pricing."

Sol 与 Luna 今日在 ChatGPT Work 与 Codex 面向 Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise/Edu 开放，同时上 API；Free 与 Go 用户可在桌面端试用 Luna。若你在做成本敏感的 agent 流水线，这是重新跑一遍单价/延迟基准的时机；官方称缓存与推理效率也有优化，值得实测而非只看公告。

02 大佬官方

OpenAI 公布"前沿节奏"独立评估框架，列出四个优先评估领域

"As part of our efforts to pace the frontier, we're committed to supporting independent assessments with deep levels of access across training, evaluation, and deployment." / "That access should enable third party assessors to challenge our assumptions, identify risks we may have missed, and reach their own conclusions about the effectiveness of our safeguards."

实用点

这是继此前"模型失准披露框架"之后，OpenAI 在第三方评估上的又一次公开表态。对做合规、安全评估或 agent 治理的团队，值得关注其"深度访问"边界如何落地——尤其是评估方能否独立复现结论，而非只拿厂商提供的材料。

03 工具更新

Grok Build 1.0.41 发布，Grok 4.7 进入 Grok Build

"SpaceXAI just released a major Grok Build update, with Grok 4.7 now available in Grok Build." / "The update improves crash recovery, prompt handling, navigation, configuration reliability and overall subagent performance. It also adds new subagent controls, configurable request limits and long-reasoning reminders."

实用点

对 agent 工程侧较实用的几点：新增 subagent 模型继承设置（持久化到 config.toml，尊重 managed/overlay 层）；可按模型配置请求体大小上限以匹配 provider HTTP body cap；可开启 long-reasoning reminder 在长隐藏推理后提醒模型。修复项里包括 agent frontmatter mcpServers 的 header/URL 覆盖在配置重载与 agent 切换后不再丢失——如果你踩过 MCP 配置被覆盖的坑，这条值得升级验证。

04 工具更新

Grok Bot 与 Connectors 上车 Tesla

"Cars used to just take you to work. Now they do some of it on the way. You can now use Grok Bot and Connectors in your Tesla."

实用点

接入路径为：在 Tesla 内登录 Grok app → 在手机端 Grok app 或网页添加 Connectors → 订阅 SuperGrok 以使用 Grok Bot。属于分发渠道扩张，对开发者而言意味着车载场景可能成为新的 agent 入口，但目前未见开放第三方接入的说明。

05 行业趋势

Google 威胁情报持续发现公开网页中的间接 prompt injection

"Google's threat intel keeps finding indirect prompt injection sitting in public web pages." / "If your model browses, that page is part of the control plane. Input validation isn't a nice-to-have — it's the stop before the tool call."

实用点

与已发过的 ASD agentic harness 指引同一逻辑：控制点应放在模型外层。对做浏览型 agent 的团队，这条的实际含义是——把抓取到的网页内容当作不可信输入，在工具调用前设一道校验/白名单，而不是指望模型自己识别注入。

06 行业趋势

Brevo 供应链事件经被入侵的 Cloudflare 访问波及 10 万+ 网站

"A Brevo supply-chain hit rode compromised Cloudflare access into 100k+ websites." / "If they can change what loads on your customers' pages, you inherited their blast radius the day you signed the contract."

实用点

老问题的新案例：第三方只要能在你的页面上加载脚本，其被攻破就等于你的被攻破。对工程团队的实际动作是定期审计第三方脚本的加载路径与权限边界，而不是只做一次性的 vendor onboarding 检查表。
