01 行业趋势

Claude Opus 5.5 (Max) 登顶 Code Arena WebDev，1818 分改写性价比前沿

"Claude Opus 5.5 (Max) by @AnthropicAI just topped #1 in Code Arena: WebDev with 1818 pts and reshapes the Pareto frontier! This is a solid +26pt lead ahead of the next best model, GPT-6 Astra (Max) and a huge +126pt improvement over previous Opus 5 (Max) at 1692." / "Claude Opus 5.5 (Max) delivers top performance at a blended $16 per Mtoken, reshaping the Pareto frontier."

实用点

这是对上一轮"GPT-6 Sol/Luna 降价"叙事的直接对冲——前沿能力与每 token 成本同时被重定价。做模型选型的团队应把 WebDev/前端类任务重新跑一遍 A/B，而不是沿用上周结论；注意榜单为社区投票，且 Opus 5.5 的 Arena 分数"coming soon"，当前排名口径需自行核对。

02 行业趋势

Mandiant 披露：攻击者劫持活跃 AI 编码助手会话，投毒 PyPI 包窃取 OAuth，波及约 100 个仓库

"Mandiant (THN): attacker hijacks an active AI coding-assistant session at a SaaS shop. Poisoned pkg recommended + accepted. Infostealer via PyPI. OAuth stolen. Shai-Hulud ~100 repos." / "Treat assistants + MCP as privileged sessions. Who refuses before an AI-recommended dep lands?"

实用点

攻击面已从"模型输出不可信"前移到"助手会话本身是特权身份"。工程上可落地的动作：把 AI 推荐的依赖安装纳入人工/策略审批闸门，MCP 与助手会话按特权凭证管理（与近期 NIST/CISA IR 8587 的 agent 令牌思路一致），并对 OAuth 授权做最小化与轮换。

03 大佬官方

OpenAI 发布 MentalHealthBench：80+ 临床医生参与构建的开放心理健康对话基准

"We're demonstrating how frontier models have continued to improve in realistic mental health conversations with MentalHealthBench. This new open benchmark was built with input from more than 80 mental health clinicians." / "Most mental health benchmarks focus on emergency situations. MentalHealthBench is designed to cover the full spectrum of mental health conversations that people bring to AI - from everyday support to more acute crisis scenarios."

实用点

对做 eval 的团队，这是一个"非应急场景全覆盖"的基准设计样本——值得借鉴其分层方式（日常支持 → 急性危机）来重构自家安全评测集。开放方法学意味着可复现、可对比，适合作为安全对齐方向的基线之一。

04 工具更新

ChatGPT Voice 升级：可调用邮件/日历/Slack 插件，支持 GPT-6 Astra、Sol、Luna，并进入 ChatGPT Work

"We heard you loud and clear. ChatGPT Voice can now: - Use plugins like your email, calendar, and Slack. - Be powered by GPT-6 Astra, Sol, and Luna. - Be used in ChatGPT Work on web and mobile, so you can create docs, decks, sites, and spreadsheets or tackle complex tasks in the browser, just by talking."

实用点

语音从"对话入口"变成"带工具权限的执行入口"，这直接扩大了 prompt injection 的可触达面（语音触发 + 邮件/日历/Slack 读写）。若团队在用语音入口，建议先明确哪些插件权限允许在语音会话中生效。

06 关注动态

GPT-6 Sol 社区口碑分化：更便宜但"略差"，被指是较弱的 OpenAI 发布

"GPT-6 Sol is one of worst OpenAI drops, and as much as i love them i have to admit this.. I really expect some huge jump of quality that would make it almost as good as Astra, while a lot cheaper, and instead we got a lot cheaper + a little worse.. I hope GPT-6.1 series changes this, and Sol will be frontier again"

实用点

这是对已发布主题（GPT-6 Sol/Luna 降价 50%）的后续反馈，非重复。提示"降价换能力"的策略在部分开发者中未获认可；选型时不要只看价格，Sol 与 Astra 的能力差需按任务实测。属个人观点，非官方结论。

07 行业趋势

Governance-as-Code：把 43 条可机器校验的 EU AI Act 条款放进 CI

"Governance-as-Code (arxiv): 43 machine-checkable EU AI Act criteria in CI — Article-indexed evidence, Art.25 deployer layers only." / "Cool. A green pipeline still isn't a named Tuesday stop. Who owns interrupt when the retrieval corpus drifts and Art.43 never fires?"

实用点

合规正在从文档走向 CI 检查项，对做企业级 agent 交付的团队有直接参考价值。但原帖也点出关键缺口：CI 绿灯 ≠ 有人负责中断，检索语料漂移这类运行时问题不在条款覆盖内——治理代码化解决"可查"，不解决"谁按下停止键"。

08 工具更新

ScienceBuddy：免费 AI 科研工作台，可检索论文并回溯每一步调用

"刚发现一个免费 AI 科研工作台 ScienceBuddy……它给我找出了 8 篇论文整理成表、用大白话讲了 Jev 跟 ChatGPT 有啥不一样，还给了几个适合新手的毕设方向。我挑了两篇 AI 搜的论文去 arXiv 核对，都对得上，AI 的每一步调用都透明可查，能翻回去确认。"
