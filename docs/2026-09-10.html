"98% of GPT-6 Astra at 1.4% of the cost is hard to ignore. I'm curious how DeepSeek V4.1 Flash holds up in real agent loops—especially long-context retrieval and tool-calling, not just the benchmark."

03 大佬官方

OpenAI 公开"防御工厂"架构与实战手册

"We mobilized 250+ people to strengthen our defenses across hundreds of systems... We're sharing what we learned, the architecture, and a practical playbook so you can build your own Defense Factory: a continuous loop where AI agents find vulnerabilities, validate them, and verify that fixes work."

实用点

可直接借鉴的是"发现—验证—确认修复"的闭环 agent 编排模式，适用于内部安全扫描、依赖漏洞治理等场景；把验证与修复确认做成独立环节，能减少误报带来的无效工单。

"Paul Christiano, founder of the Alignment Research Center, is joining the OpenAI Foundation Board and its Safety and Security Committee... Paul will also serve as a non-voting observer on the OpenAI Group PBC Board."

05 大佬官方

OpenAI 就数学证明事件说明数据来源边界

"We (the researchers and the agents) did not see any of their work through any means until they released it publicly — in particular, no specific user data was accessed in order to solve this problem. While unlikely, we cannot rule out that de-identified data derived from their usage of our products helped improve our models."

实用点

这是一次关于"模型能力来源"的公开边界声明，措辞谨慎（承认无法完全排除去标识化数据的影响）。对做数据合规、用户数据治理的团队，可作为"能力归因披露"的参考文本。
