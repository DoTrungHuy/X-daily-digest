01 工具更新

Google 发布 Gemini Robotics 2 与 Gemini Robotics ER 2，主打全身控制与多机器人协作

"Introducing Gemini Robotics 2 from @GoogleDeepMind, the intelligence layer powering the next generation of truly adaptable robots. This major advance unlocks intelligent whole-body control, advanced dexterity, and even multi-robot collaboration 🤯."

实用点

对做机器人或具身智能的开发者是重大参考——ER 2 作为高层“大脑”负责观察、推理、协调 VLA 模型并跟踪进度，这种分层架构（reasoning model + vision-language-action model）值得借鉴到复杂多步 Agent 任务设计中。

02 工具更新

Google 连发 Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite、3.6 Flash、3.5 Flash Cyber 三款模型，覆盖速度、成本与安全

"Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is our fastest, most cost-effective model optimized for high-speed agentic workflows" / "Gemini 3.6 Flash delivers faster, more accurate performance across tasks while using significantly fewer tokens" / "Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is our new, cost-effective model optimized for finding and fixing software vulnerabilities at scale, available exclusively to governments and trusted partners"

实用点

Flash-Lite 适合高吞吐 agentic 场景，3.6 Flash 主打 token 效率，Cyber 版则专攻漏洞挖掘修复（仅限政府与可信伙伴）。做 Agent 选型时可重点关注前两者的性价比。

"With the help of GPT-5.6 Sol, we have made leaps in efficiency. Today, we are passing those gains on in the API with lower prices for Luna and Terra, and providing faster performance to Sol."

这是对已发布降价消息的官方确认与补充——Sol 的推理效率提升直接转化为 API 价格下调，做成本敏感型应用可重新评估 Luna/Terra 的性价比。
