“I built it! 🍰 Infinite Slop... An infinite and interactive AI generated live stream of slop that goes on forever and ever. Anything that you write in the chat is generated next and AI will try to connect it to the previous video so there's an actual sequence and story line.”

06 行业趋势

2027 年约 15GW AI 算力可能“无法通电”：电力之外，配套基础设施是更大瓶颈

“Consensus estimate is that ~15GW of AI compute produced in 2027 cannot be turned on in 2027. This is harder than just finding power, as you also need to build out all the transformers, wiring, liquid-cooling, (massive) chillers & complex networking.”

实用点

对做 AI 基础设施或数据中心相关工作的工程师，这提醒了算力供给的瓶颈远不止电力。变压器、冷却、网络等配套环节的交付周期，可能成为决定 2027 年算力实际可用量的关键变量。对规划长期算力预算的团队有参考意义。

07 关注动态

levelsio 分享“人生管理”工作流：Notion 看板 + agent 自动更新状态

“finally found a decent 'life management' approach: is a notion board w/ 'backlog', 'block on me', 'waiting', 'complete', agents update it as tasks complete. Then i have a daily automation with @bot which the status of items, updates them, and pings me on what i need to do.”
