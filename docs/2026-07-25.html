Anthropic 发布 Claude Opus 5，号称接近 Fable 5 前沿智能但价格减半

"Introducing Claude Opus 5. It's a thoughtful and proactive model that comes close to the frontier intelligence of Fable 5 at half the price." / "On several coding and knowledge work evaluations, Opus 5 is the new state-of-the-art."

实用点

开发者可评估该模型在 coding 和知识工作上的性价比，尤其适合成本敏感但需要接近前沿能力的 agent 场景。Pieter Levels 已验证：Opus 5 一次提示生成交互式 3D 生物标记扫描页面，而 Fable 因安全机制拒绝执行，说明 Opus 5 在创造性/硬编码任务上更灵活。

OpenAI 公布 Hugging Face 事件初步声明：将发布技术报告

"We recognize there are a lot of questions and speculative details circulating related to the Hugging Face incident. This is an unprecedented incident, and we think it marks an important moment for AI safety. We are still conducting a thorough review... Once the review is complete, we plan to publish a technical report of our learnings in the coming weeks."

实用点

这是继 Sam Altman 确认安全事件后 OpenAI 的首次正式声明。对安全研究者/agent 开发者而言，需关注后续报告中的具体攻击面和技术细节，可能影响 agent 沙箱设计。
